Cubentia

Cubentia is an offline Android tutor. It teaches a beginner to solve a 3×3 cube, one step at a time.

Learners can practice on a virtual cube on screen, or follow along with a real cube in hand.

No account, no internet is needed

Cubentia works without an account and without an internet connection. Everything it teaches is already in the app.
