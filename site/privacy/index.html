Privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026

This policy covers the Cubentia app for Android and this website. Cubentia is made by Exxerpro Solutions, S.A. de C.V. (published on Google Play as Abel Briones).

The app this policy covers has the Android package name com.exxerpro.cubentia.

In short

  • Cubentia collects no personal data and shares none. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.
  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • There are no ads.
  • There is no analytics and no usage tracking.
  • There is no crash reporting.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries.

Permissions

  • The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything over the network.
  • The app asks for no runtime permission. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or your files.
  • The app does not allow backup, so your progress is not copied to a cloud backup or to another device.

Your progress

Your progress is stored only on your device, in storage that only the app can read. Uninstalling the app removes it.

You can also erase your progress at any time with Reset progress in the app’s settings.

Learners of any age

This policy applies to Learners of any age. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no scripts and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security. Read the GitHub General Privacy Statement to learn how GitHub handles that data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page shows the new text and a new effective date.
